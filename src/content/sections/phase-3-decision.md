Phase 3 ran continuously from late 2023 through to December 2025, when two factors combined to trigger a pivot:

1. **Weak commercial demand** for SCV as a standalone product had been apparent for some time.
2. **Intermedia's acquisition of Gordon and Gotch** — the company behind Myriad — in December 2025 made becoming CRM agnostic across all core products an urgent business priority, not just a longer-term aspiration.

The Phase 3 architecture was sound. The pivot was not a rejection of the approach — it was the acquisition that forced the question of what to build next. Phase 4 applies the same Harbour and Crane pattern to a specific, high-value user need: promo code creation.
