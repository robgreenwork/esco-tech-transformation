We halted exclusive development on the SCV product and pivoted the strategic focus. Two factors drove this decision:

1. **Weak commercial demand** for SCV as a standalone product.
2. **The Intermedia acquisition of Gordon and Gotch**, the company behind Myriad, which made becoming CRM agnostic across all core products an urgent business priority, not just a longer-term aspiration.

The Phase 3 architecture was sound. The pivot was not a rejection of the approach, it was an expansion of it. Phase 4 applies the same Harbour and Crane pattern to the full Subscriptions, Memberships, and Charities business.
