Phase 3 ran continuously from late 2023 through to July 2025, when two factors combined to trigger a pivot:

1. **Weak commercial demand** for SCV as a standalone product had been apparent for some time.
2. **Intermedia's acquisition of Gordon and Gotch** (the company behind Myriad) — news of which broke in July 2025, with the transaction completing in September 2025 — made becoming CRM agnostic across all core products an urgent business priority, not just a longer-term aspiration. The pivot was actioned in July 2025.

The Phase 3 architecture was sound. The pivot was not a rejection of the approach; it was the acquisition that forced the question of what to build next. [Phase 4](/phases/phase-4) applies the same Harbour and Crane pattern to a specific, high-value user need: promo code creation.
