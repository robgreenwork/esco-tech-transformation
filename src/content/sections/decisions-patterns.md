Looking across the four major decisions, a consistent pattern emerges: each phase produced evidence that the current approach was either unaffordable, technically unsound, or commercially unviable -- and each decision was made to address that evidence directly rather than persist with a failing hypothesis.

The recurring tension throughout the programme has been **momentum versus certainty**: the pressure to keep delivering and prove the concept quickly, versus the desire to make fully informed choices about tools and architecture. The fourth decision (proceeding with MongoDB despite the external warnings) is the clearest example of momentum winning -- an accepted, documented risk rather than an oversight.
