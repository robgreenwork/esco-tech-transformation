Every alternative system evaluated was significantly more expensive than Myriad. Internal teams had mixed feelings: Customer Service valued Myriad's rigid controls, while Client Management found it clunky and limiting.

Most critically, we discovered that our bespoke external systems were so tightly coupled to Myriad that a direct "lift and shift" migration was impossible. A direct replacement would require unpicking years of custom integrations before any new system could be brought in.
