The programme is paused. [Phase 4](/phases/phase-4) was demoed to Anthem in April 2026 and received an ambivalent response — not enough commercial enthusiasm to justify continuing on the current path. Louise made the call to pause.

The immediate priority is the **RFP**: returning to the [Phase 2](/phases/phase-2) question with three years of additional context. The core question is whether ESco needs to build custom software at all, or whether the right off-the-shelf subscription management platform now exists at an affordable price point. The Myriad contract ending and rising licence costs make this the right moment to reassess.

Two things should run in parallel with the RFP:

1. **Resume the Microservices Audit** — this was left partially complete and is directly relevant to any migration or rebuild decision. It also connects to the EOS process component and should not be allowed to stall further.
2. **Revisit the full cloud migration question** — parked during [Phase 4](/phases/phase-4) but very much live again. The RFP outcome will determine what migration path makes sense, but options should be kept open.

The [Phase 3](/phases/phase-3) and [Phase 4](/phases/phase-4) technical work is not wasted — the architecture is proven at POC level and the learnings are documented here. If the RFP leads back to a build path, much of this groundwork remains valid.
