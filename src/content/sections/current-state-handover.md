The programme is at an active pivot point. [Phase 3](/phases/phase-3) proved the technical concept. [Phase 4](/phases/phase-4) is the strategic expansion of that concept to the core business. The two immediate priorities for whoever picks this up are:

1. Complete the [Phase 4](/phases/phase-4) narrow proof of concept (Books/Backs and Comm Docs microservices) to validate the architecture at the Subscriptions/Memberships/Charities level.
2. Resolve the open build-or-buy decision on the Crane, informed by what the PoC reveals about integration complexity and volume.

The Microservices Audit will run in parallel and is important context for both decisions above.
