Loise decided to pause the programme in April 2026 following the Anthem demo. The feature worked technically, but the client response did not provide sufficient confidence to continue at the same level of investment. The pause was not a rejection of the underlying architecture; it was a recognition that the commercial signal needed to justify the next step had not materialised.

In hindsight, the decision by the senior leadership team to pursue promo code creation as the Phase 4 focus was probably the wrong call. The choice was influenced by feedback from a client the team was pitching for and ultimately did not win which means the strategic direction of the phase was shaped by a commercial outcome that never arrived.

The [Current State](/current-state) page (May 2026 onwards) reflects this pause: the programme is on hold while the business considers its options.
