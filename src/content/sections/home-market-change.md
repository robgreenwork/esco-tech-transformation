The market in which Esco operates has undergone significant structural change since 2022. The specialist magazine publishing sector, which forms the core of Esco's client base, has faced mounting pressure from declining print circulation and the growing complexity of managing audiences across both print and digital channels simultaneously. UK magazine publishers have seen revenue forecast to shrink at a compound annual rate of 5.2%, with print circulation falling 12.4% in 2023 alone ([IBISWorld](https://img.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/industry/magazine-publishers/3475)), driving publishers to demand more from their technology and fulfilment partners. By the end of 2024, 55% of B2B publishing companies had transitioned to subscription-based revenue models, a 12-point rise compared to 2022 ([Global Growth Insights](https://www.globalgrowthinsights.com/market-reports/b2b-publishing-market-100130)), reflecting a broader shift from single-copy and print-first thinking to a recurring, audience-centric model that requires more sophisticated data management and renewal logic.

Alongside this shift in publisher behaviour, the competitive landscape for subscription management has evolved. A new generation of SaaS platforms has entered the market, positioning themselves as modern alternatives to the traditional bureau model. Pelcro, for example, was founded in 2016 out of frustration with what its founders saw as a lack of truly modern magazine subscription management services ([Pelcro](https://www.get.pelcro.com/magazine-subscription-management-software)), and has since grown to offer self-serve subscription management, paywall control and billing automation within a single platform. Darwin CX, a Toronto-based subscription and customer experience platform, has accelerated its push into the UK market, recently winning accounts including National Geographic Kids and The Chelsea Magazine Company, with publishers citing a need to consolidate fragmented systems and improve data visibility across print and digital ([PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/darwin-cx-wins-new-global-publisher-accounts-as-media-companies-prioritize-unified-subscription-data-302761952.html)). These platforms compete on flexibility, real-time data access and lower perceived overhead, putting pressure on bureau-model businesses like Esco to demonstrate the value of managed services, deep institutional knowledge and the kind of hands-on client relationship that SaaS platforms do not typically provide.
