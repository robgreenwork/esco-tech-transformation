ESco is a publishing services bureau offering subscription management, customer service, and eCommerce solutions. For many years the business relied heavily on Myriad as its core subscription management database -- a single point of failure that had accumulated significant technical debt, an outdated interface, and deeply embedded bespoke integrations that made it slow to adapt.

When the company behind Myriad (Gordon and Gotch) was acquired by Intermedia, the urgency to reduce dependency on any single CRM became critical. The Tech Transformation programme was initiated to find a path to a CRM-agnostic architecture: one where ESco could plug in new systems, cut operating costs, improve scalability, and confidently price and sell services with healthy margins.

The programme was designed to protect the core business for existing publishing clients while enabling ESco to scale into new verticals -- memberships, charities, and non-publishing subscription businesses.
