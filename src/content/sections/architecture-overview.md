In May 2022, ESco commissioned **Ivan Teage** (Harmonic Action Technologies) to carry out an external review of the technology architecture and recommend how to evolve it for the next decade. This was the starting point for the whole programme.

The review brought an outside perspective on the existing stack, the strengths and weaknesses of Myriad, and the choices available for modernisation. It set the direction that [Phase 1](/phases/phase-1) then went on to test.
